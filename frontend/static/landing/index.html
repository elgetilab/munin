Munin Research Assistant

AI-powered research assistant with access to your papers and the web. Munin automatically routes each question to the right mode: chat, coding, or research.

Open Chat Search Papers Research

What You Can Do

1

Chat with AI

Ask questions, write text, get help with code, or explore research topics. Munin automatically routes each turn to the right mode, or steer it yourself with /research, /code, or /chat.

  • General questions and writing
  • Programming and debugging
  • Literature search and analysis
2

Search Papers

Search our paper database by topic, author, year, or DOI. Results are ranked by meaning, not just keywords, so you find conceptually related work.

  • Semantic search by topic
  • Filter by author and year
  • DOI lookup and PDF download
3

Deep Research

Submit a complex research question and get a comprehensive report. Jobs run on cluster GPUs and typically take 5 to 20 minutes.

  • In-depth analysis of a topic
  • Structured report with references
  • Download as PDF or Markdown
4

Upload Papers

Contribute papers to the shared database. Uploaded PDFs are indexed and become searchable for everyone. Supports large batches with resumable uploads.

  • Add papers to the shared database
  • Upload up to 1,000 PDFs at once
  • Resumes automatically if interrupted

Meet Munin

Ask anything, and Munin automatically routes your question to the right mode.

Behind the scenes, Munin selects the best mode for each turn:

  • Chat for writing, web search, quick lookups, and brainstorming.
  • Code for programming, scientific computing, debugging, and code review.
  • Research for literature search, paper analysis, and citation exploration.

Focus your work with /research, /code, or /chat.

All assistants draw from web search, academic databases (Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex), and our internal paper collection, searchable by meaning, not just keywords.
