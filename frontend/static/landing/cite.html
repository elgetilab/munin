Cite Munin

How to credit Munin, and the language model behind it, when it helped with your research or your writing.

The short answer

Munin and its model are tools, not authors: no journal accepts an AI system as an author, because it cannot take responsibility for the work. What journals ask for instead is disclosure. In practice that is three things:

  1. A disclosure statement saying what you used, for what, and how you checked the output. It goes in Methods when Munin did research work (literature search, analysis, code), or in the journal's AI-use declaration when it only helped with writing.
  2. References to the Munin paper, to the model's own report, and to vLLM, the software that serves the model.
  3. An acknowledgment of the lab that hosts the service.

Your own usage

In Chat, open Settings and go to Credit Munin in a paper. Pick the date range of your project and you get:

  • every model that answered you in that range, with checkpoint, revision, serving software and first and last use;
  • request, conversation, Deep Research and token counts per model, and a CSV export;
  • the disclosure statement above, filled in with those models and dates, plus the matching references and BibTeX.

Only metadata is used: which model answered, when, and how many tokens. Nothing from your chats goes into it. If part of your range fell into a cluster outage, some requests may have been answered by a hosted model (Claude, through the Anthropic API); the statement lists that separately, which matters if your data-protection statement says data stayed on site.

Policies as checked on 2026-11-21. They change; the linked page is the authority.

ICMJE journalsCover letter and the relevant section of the paperSame

Science (AAAS) and ACS are not in the table because their policy pages could not be checked. Secondary sources say Science wants use noted in the cover letter and Acknowledgments, with details in Methods.

Reproducibility

  • Name the exact model, checkpoint and revision. "An LLM" or a model family is not enough.
  • Give dates. The model behind Munin is replaced from time to time, and the history is kept, so your dates identify the model.
  • Expect run-to-run variation: a locally served model's output depends on batch size and hardware even at temperature 0. Archive the outputs your results rest on, not only the prompts.
  • For clinical prediction work, see TRIPOD-LLM; for chatbot health-advice studies, the CHART statement.
